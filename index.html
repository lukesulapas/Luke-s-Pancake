    About us

    Luke’s Pancake is a food truck that moves between schools, markets, and community events in Bukidnon. We serve warm, affordable, freshly made pancakes with a local twist.

    Our Sweet Corn pancake is a nod to Bukidnon’s corn-growing heritage, and every stack is cooked only after you order it.
